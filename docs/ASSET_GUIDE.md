# Public asset guide

Only static institutional material belongs in `public/images` and public guides in `public/documents/public-guides`. Use:

- `branding/` for approved NDMU and NDMU-RMAS logos
- `school/` for official school images
- `campus/` for campus photography
- `departments/`, `backgrounds/`, `icons/`, and `defaults/` for their named public assets

Use descriptive kebab-case names such as `ndmu-main-building.jpg`, `ndmu-campus-entrance.jpg`, `ndmu-rmas-login-background.jpg`, `default-student-avatar.png`, and `default-faculty-avatar.png`. Optimize images, retain licensing/source records, and do not commit unapproved branding.

Profile photos, manuscripts, evaluation sheets, defense documents, and any confidential upload are private storage—not public assets.
