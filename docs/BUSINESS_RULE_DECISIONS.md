# Business Rule Decision Register

This register records confirmed cross-feature rules. It supplements phase-specific documentation; it does not replace repository code, migrations, policies, or tests as implementation evidence.

## Phase 18 — Research Progress Milestones

- Official progress belongs to `ResearchClassGroup`, not an individual student or document.
- The 12 NDMU stages listed in `docs/PHASE_18_RESEARCH_PROGRESS_MILESTONES.md` are authoritative.
- The owning Research Facilitator controls official state through explicit permission plus current class ownership.
- Students and assigned advisers have read-only contextual access; verified former/disbanded membership remains historical read-only.
- Weights are centrally configurable and equal by default; percentage is derived and never manually entered.
- States are Pending, In Progress, Completed, and Not Applicable. N/A requires a reason and is excluded from the denominator.
- Sequence is enforced by default. A controlled out-of-order override requires permission, explicit intent, and a reason.
- Completed/N/A corrections require reasons and preserve event history.
- Due dates are optional and audited; overdue is derived rather than stored as a lifecycle status.
- Evidence is optional, same-group restricted, and never automatically changes status.
- Document acceptance, consultation completion, and revision resolution do not automatically mutate progress.
- Phase 18 dispatches no notifications; notification behavior remains Phase 23.
